# 💼 Salary & Workforce Insights Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Problem Statement

Understanding salary distribution across different job roles and fields is essential for both individuals and organizations. This project analyzes workforce salary data to identify compensation trends, high-paying roles, and variations across different fields, enabling data-driven career and business decisions.

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

* Dataset includes:

  * Job roles / fields
  * Salary information
  * Other workforce-related attributes
* Data was cleaned, transformed, and structured for analysis using Power BI

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

* Average Salary
* Maximum & Minimum Salary
* Salary Distribution by Role
* Field-wise Salary Comparison
* Role-wise Contribution to Total Compensation

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Certain job roles and fields offer significantly higher average salaries, indicating strong market demand
* Salary distribution is uneven, with a small number of roles contributing to the highest salary brackets
* Noticeable variation exists between minimum and maximum salaries, suggesting experience and specialization impact earnings
* Some fields show stable salary ranges, while others have high variability

---

## 🧠 Business / Career Impact

* Helps individuals identify **high-paying career paths**
* Supports organizations in understanding **compensation benchmarks**
* Enables better **workforce planning and salary structuring**
* Assists in identifying **salary gaps across roles and fields**
